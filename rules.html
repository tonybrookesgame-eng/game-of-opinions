How It Works

Game of Opinions is a weekly football-management challenge where your XI, your weekly predictions, and your long-term season calls all feed into a single leaderboard.

Pick Your XI

Choose 11 players from 11 different clubs and build around the one-club rule.

Weekly Calls

Back your first-goal club and minute, then try the Bet Builder for extra points.

Score Big

Appearances, goals, assists, clean sheets, and cards all feed into your total.

Think Long Term

Season picks add a bigger end-of-campaign swing once you submit them.

The Golden Rule: You can only select one player from any one Premier League club. Every team is a puzzle, and every week is a fresh test of judgment.
No Real Money: This is a virtual points game for fun. The Bet Builder uses in-game points only and does not involve real-world gambling or cash prizes.

1. Building Your XI

  • One player per club: You must pick 11 players from 11 different clubs.
  • Goalkeeper rule: Goalkeeper picks are made at club level, so your keeper selection counts as that club’s entry for the week.
  • No budget: There is no transfer market or price tag. You can pick any player you like, but that usually means sacrificing another player from the same club.
  • Formations: You can switch between standard shapes before the deadline, with 4-4-2 as the default starting point.
  • Team name: Once you save a team name, it is kept for that manager and remains part of your profile.

2. Weekly Picks & The Bet Builder

Every gameweek has three ways to score points: your selected XI, your weekly picks, and your Bet Builder prediction.

Weekly Picks

  • Club to score first: Pick the club you think will open the scoring. 10 PTS
  • Minute of first goal: Predict the minute the first goal of the weekend will arrive. Minutes 1-90 are selectable; 90+ is grouped as Other. 40 PTS

The Bet Builder

The Bet Builder is a weekly risk/reward call. The admin sets the odds for the market each week, and the current live UI shows them as an odds value such as 16/1 or 30/1.

  • Stake: It costs 5 PTS to play.
  • YES pick: If the bet lands, you win 5 × odds points. If it fails, you lose 30 points.
  • NO pick: If the bet fails, you win 30 points. If it lands, you lose 5 × odds points.

3. Weekly Scoring

Your selected XI earns points from real-life match events. The current scoring values are:

Position Scoring
Goalkeepers Appearance 1 • Clean Sheet 5 • Penalty Save 5 • Goal 20 • Assist 10 • Yellow -2 • Red -6
Defenders Appearance 1 • Clean Sheet 5 • Goal 6 • Assist 6 • Yellow -1 • Red -5
Midfielders Appearance 1 • Clean Sheet 2 • Goal 5 • Assist 5 • Yellow -1 • Red -5
Strikers Appearance 1 • Goal 4 • Assist 5 • Yellow -1 • Red -5

Clean sheets are a big differentiator, but the scoring is still designed to reward participation and attacking output rather than only defensive clean sheets.

4. Season Picks

Season picks are long-term calls that are submitted once and then scored at the end of the campaign.

  • Premier League Winner 25 PTS
  • FA Cup Winner 50 PTS
  • Top Scorer 25 PTS
  • Top Assists 50 PTS
  • Most Clean Sheets 50 PTS
  • First Hat-Trick 50 PTS
  • First Manager Sacked 50 PTS
  • Most Yellow Cards 50 PTS
  • Most Red Cards 75 PTS
  • Bottom 3 Finishers (Relegation) 40 PTS each correct team

5. League Position & Deadlines

The leaderboard is built from your total points across the season. A gameweek is processed once the admin publishes the scores, and the game then reopens for the next cycle.

  • Locking: The gameweek locks automatically two hours before the first kickoff of the fixture list.
  • Editing: You can still adjust your XI, weekly picks, and Bet Builder before the lock.
  • After scoring: Once results are published, the game reopens for the next week.
  • Season picks: These are treated as long-term predictions and are not changed on a weekly basis.

6. AI Scout Integration

The AI Scout opinions are there to spark debate and give you a second lens on your picks. They are useful for context, but they are still just opinions and should be weighed alongside your own judgment.

7. Fair Play

Integrity matters. Any attempt to exploit system bugs, create duplicate accounts, or game the scoring flow will result in removal from the league.
