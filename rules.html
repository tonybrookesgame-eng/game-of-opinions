How It Works

Game of Opinions is a football management game built on the belief that pure football knowledge should trump spreadsheets and template teams.

The Golden Rule: You can only select ONE player from any single Premier League club. Every team is a puzzle.
No Real Money: Game of Opinions is a virtual points-based game for entertainment purposes only. The "Bet Builder" uses in-game points and does not involve real-world currency, gambling, or prizes with monetary value.

1. Building Your XI

Squad Constraints

  • One Player Per Club: You must select 11 players from 11 different clubs. Note: Selecting a club for your Goalkeeper counts as that club's selection for the week.
  • Goalkeeper Flexibility: To mitigate the volatility of selection headaches, Goalkeeper picks are made at the **Club level**. Your team earns points for the position’s performance regardless of which individual starts, keeping your defensive strategy agnostic to late squad rotations or injury withdrawals.
  • No Budget: There are no player prices. If you want Haaland, you can have him—but you lose access to every other Man City player.
  • Formations: You can choose from standard formations (4-4-2, 4-3-3, 3-5-2, 3-4-3, 4-5-1).

2. Weekly Match Predictions

Every week, your knowledge is tested on specific match outcomes alongside your player selections.

Weekly Picks

  • Club to Score First: Pick the club you think will open the scoring across the weekend fixtures. 10 PTS
  • Minute of First Goal: Predict the exact minute the first goal will be scored in the featured match. Minutes 1-90 are selectable. Any goal scored in stoppage time (90+) is categorized as "Other". 40 PTS

The Bet Builder (Risk & Reward)

This is where leagues are won or lost. Every week, a "Game Of Opinions Bet Builder" is presented at fixed 16/1 odds. You must decide if it will land (YES) or fail (NO).

  • The Stake: It costs 5 PTS to play.
  • YES Selection: If the bet lands, you win 5 × 16 + 5 = 85 PTS. If it fails, you lose 5 × 6 + 5 = 35 PTS.
  • NO Selection: If the bet fails, you win 35 PTS. If the bet lands, you lose 85 PTS.
  • Note: This asymmetric payoff structure creates different risk/reward profiles for each choice at 16/1 fixed odds.

3. Season Predictions

Long-term vision pays off. These picks are locked at the start of the season (or when you join) and provide a huge point injection at the end of the campaign.

  • PL Winner 25 PTS | FA Cup Winner 50 PTS
  • Top Scorer 25 PTS | Top Assists 50 PTS
  • Most Clean Sheets 50 PTS | First Hat-Trick 50 PTS
  • First Manager Sacked 50 PTS | Most Yellow Cards 50 PTS
  • Most Red Cards (Team) 75 PTS
  • Bottom 3 (Relegation) 40 PTS Per Correct Team

4. Scoring System

Points are awarded based on real-world performance. (Specific player point values to be finalized).

Standard Scoring Categories:

  • All Players: Appearance, Goals, Assists, Yellow/Red Cards.
  • Defenders/Keepers: Clean Sheets, Goals Conceded.

7. Position Scoring Breakdown

To help you master the game, here is exactly how each role earns points:

Goalkeepers (Club Level)

  • 1 PT Appearance | 5 PTS Clean Sheet | 5 PTS Penalty Save
  • 10 PTS Assist | 20 PTS Goal
  • -2 PTS Yellow Card | -6 PTS Red Card

Defenders

  • 1 PT Appearance | 5 PTS Clean Sheet
  • 6 PTS Goal | 6 PTS Assist
  • -1 PT Yellow Card | -5 PTS Red Card

Midfielders

  • 1 PT Appearance | 2 PTS Clean Sheet
  • 5 PTS Goal | 5 PTS Assist
  • -1 PT Yellow Card | -5 PTS Red Card

Strikers

  • 1 PT Appearance | 4 PTS Goal | 5 PTS Assist
  • -1 PT Yellow Card | -5 PTS Red Card

8. League Standings & Tie-breakers

The Global Leaderboard is updated after every Gameweek is processed. If two or more managers finish the season on identical points, the tie is broken by:

  • 1. Highest GW Score: The manager with the highest single-week points total.
  • 2. Minute Mastery: Most correct "Goal Minute" predictions throughout the season.
  • 3. The Legend Status: The Top 3 managers are awarded the 🥇, 🥈, and 🥉 medals.

9. Postponed & Abandoned Matches

Football is unpredictable. Our policy on match changes is as follows:

  • Postponements: If a match is moved outside of the current Gameweek window, players from those clubs will score 0 PTS.
  • Abandoned Matches: If a match is abandoned after kickoff, only the points earned up until the whistle blew will be counted.
  • Rescheduled Matches: Points are only awarded for the Gameweek in which the match actually takes place.

10. Fair Play

The Manager's Office is a place of integrity. Any attempt to exploit system bugs or create multiple accounts to "farm" predictions will result in a permanent ban from the league.

5. AI Scout Integration

Use the "Opinion AI" icons on the Season and Team pages to get insights from legends like R9 or Fergie. Their analysis is based on current form and historical data, but remember—it's just an opinion.

6. Deadlines & Changes

  • XI Changes: You can change your team as much as you like until the first kickoff of the Gameweek.
  • Weekly Picks: Locked at the same time as your team.
  • Season Picks: Locked permanently once submitted.
